How to answer

  1. Answer for what is actually operating, not what is drafted, intended, or sitting in a folder.
  2. Use Don’t know honestly. It never counts as a low answer, and a lot of them is a finding in its own right.
  3. Use Not applicable only when the question genuinely cannot apply to your institution.
  4. Have several people complete it separately. Where informed colleagues describe the same thing differently, that disagreement is the finding.
  5. Click a selected answer again to clear it.
About this instrument

This instrument assesses readiness against the five Phase 1 components only. Use-case inventory including shadow AI, human-in-the-loop rules, and appeal rules are deliberately out of scope — they sit in Phase 2, above the floor this measures. Framework note: the components and their build order come from the Minimum Viable Governance framework by Strategies by Design Group. They align with the GOVERN function of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0; this instrument is not a NIST conformance assessment. Cut points are provisional SME judgment pending a formal review.
